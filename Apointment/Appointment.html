Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my first consultation? +
Just yourself and an open mind. If you have specific inspiration or a garment you'd like to match, feel free to bring it along.
How long does a bespoke tailoring session take? +
Initial consultations usually take 45–60 minutes. The full crafting process typically requires 3–5 fittings over several weeks.
What does "Fashionista" styling include? +
This is our VIP tier. It includes full personal styling, event logistics coordination, a luxury vehicle assignment, and an envoy/entourage to accompany your arrival.
Can I reschedule my appointment? +
Yes, you can reschedule via the confirmation email you receive, provided it's at least 24 hours in advance.
